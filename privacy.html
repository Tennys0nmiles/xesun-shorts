Privacy Policy

XESUN Shorts Uploader · effective October 12, 2026

XESUN Shorts Uploader is a private tool the XESUN team uses to post gameplay videos of our Roblox games to our own YouTube channel and our own TikTok and Instagram accounts (@bloxesun). It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with information.

Who uses it

Only the XESUN team, signing in with the Google account that owns the XESUN YouTube channel. The tool refuses to post to any other channel.

What it accesses

Through YouTube API Services, with the permissions granted at sign-in, it can:

  • upload videos to the signed-in channel (youtube.upload);
  • read that channel's name and ID, and the status of the videos it uploaded (youtube.readonly), to confirm it is posting to the XESUN channel and that each video went live.

Through the TikTok Content Posting API, signed in as @bloxesun, it can read the account's basic profile (user.info.basic) and send videos to the account's inbox as drafts (video.upload).

Through the Instagram API, signed in as @bloxesun, it can read the account's basic profile (instagram_business_basic) and publish Reels to it (instagram_business_content_publish).

It does not read, collect or store information about anyone else: not viewers, not commenters, not other accounts.

What it stores, and where

The sign-in token and a log of the videos it posted are kept only on the XESUN team's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, to post our videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token from our computer also stops all access.

Deleting data

The tool keeps no data about anyone but our own accounts. To remove its access to TikTok, open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; for Instagram, open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. The team then deletes the stored tokens from its computer.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the XESUN community on Roblox or the XESUN YouTube channel.
