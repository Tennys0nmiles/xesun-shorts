XESUN Shorts Uploader · effective October 12, 2026

XESUN Shorts Uploader is a private tool the XESUN team uses to post short gameplay videos of our own Roblox games to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and nobody outside the XESUN team may sign in to it.

Use

  • It only posts videos the XESUN team made, of games the XESUN team made, to accounts the XESUN team owns.
  • On TikTok it sends each video to the account's inbox as a draft; a person on the team reviews it and finishes the post in the TikTok app.
  • Every post must follow the rules of the platform it goes to, including their community guidelines and their rules for branded and AI-labelled content.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for the team's own use, with no warranty of any kind.

Platforms

Using the tool also means following the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the Instagram Terms of Use.
