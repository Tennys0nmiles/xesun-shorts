XESUN Shorts Uploader

A private tool used by the XESUN team.

XESUN makes Roblox games, including Slime Snatchers and FITCHECK. This tool posts short gameplay videos of those games to our own YouTube channel, XESUN (@BLOXESUN), and to our own TikTok and Instagram accounts (@bloxesun).

It runs on the XESUN team's own computer and only ever posts to XESUN's own accounts. It is not a public service, and nobody outside the team signs in to it.
